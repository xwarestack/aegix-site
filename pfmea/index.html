The method

From the process flow to the failure chain, step by step.

Aegix follows the AIAG & VDA steps for a process analysis. Each step starts from what the previous one produced, so nothing is entered twice.

1

Structure

Process item, process steps by operation number, and the work elements of each step.

2

Functions

What each step and work element has to achieve, with the characteristics it has to hold.

3

Failures

The failure mode at the step, its effect on the product and its cause at the work element.

4

Risk

Severity, occurrence and detection, and the Action Priority they give.

5

Optimisation

Prevention and detection actions with an owner and a due date, then the new ratings.

Work elements

Every step, broken down by what can go wrong.

Each process step fans out to its work elements, tagged by the category they are analysed under. A work element with no function or failure mode stands out, so a gap shows before the review, not during it.

Man

The operator and the skills the step depends on.

Machine

Equipment, tools and fixtures.

Material

Parts and materials entering the step.

Method

The work instruction and the sequence.

Environment

Conditions around the step, such as temperature and cleanliness.

Measurement

Gauges and checks that confirm the result.

The process structure as a diagram. The work element outlined in red has no function and no failure mode against it yet.

Control plan

The characteristics each step has to hold, in the analysis.

Product and process characteristics sit on the process function that has to hold them, so a failure mode is analysed against the characteristic it puts at risk.

The PFMEA and the control plan describe the same line.

Process step
Op 10, press the impeller onto the shaft.
Function
Seat the impeller at the specified depth.
Characteristic
Press-in depth within tolerance.
Failure mode
Impeller not fully seated.
Cause
Press force set too low.
