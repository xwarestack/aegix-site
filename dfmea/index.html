The method

From the architecture to the failure chain, step by step.

Aegix follows the AIAG & VDA steps for a design analysis. Each step starts from what the previous one produced, so nothing is entered twice.

1

Structure

System, subsystems, components and the interfaces between them.

2

Functions

What each element has to do, with the requirement it has to meet.

3

Failures

The failure mode of the focus element, its effect one level up and its cause one level down.

4

Risk

Severity, occurrence and detection, and the Action Priority they give.

5

Optimisation

Prevention and detection actions with an owner and a due date, then the new ratings.

Requirements

Every failure mode knows which requirement it threatens.

Requirements come from your ALM, such as Codebeamer or Polarion, and keep their id. Each function carries the requirement it meets, so a failure mode points back to it, and forward to the verification that covers it.

When a requirement changes, you can see which part of the DFMEA depends on it.

Requirement
Coolant outlet temperature stays below 95 °C at the continuous duty point.
Function
Keep the powertrain within its thermal operating window.
Failure mode
Coolant outlet temperature exceeds the limit.
Cause
Temperature sensing failure.
Verification
The test that covers the requirement.

Questions

Common questions.

What is a Design FMEA?

A Design FMEA (DFMEA) analyses how a product design can fail, what the effects would be and what could cause them, so the risks are reduced before the design is released. The AIAG & VDA handbook describes it in seven steps, from planning to documenting the results.

How does Aegix help with a DFMEA?

Aegix builds the DFMEA structure from your requirements and specifications, drafts functions and failure modes for your engineers to review, rates each failure chain with Action Priority, and tracks the actions until the risk is re-rated.

Which method does Aegix follow?

The AIAG & VDA FMEA method, with severity, occurrence and detection ratings and Action Priority. Templates are configurable for other terminology, rating scales or worksheet layouts.

Does Aegix connect to our ALM?

Yes. Aegix reads requirements from your ALM, such as Codebeamer or Polarion, and keeps each one linked to its source. FMEA actions can go to your task tool, such as Jira.
