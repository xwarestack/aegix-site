FMEA shouldn't start with a blank table.

The information needed for an FMEA already exists — spread across system specifications, requirements, architecture documents, design descriptions, previous FMEAs, test documentation and ALM systems. Engineers then spend hours finding it, interpreting it and transferring it into another structure.

A structured starting point instead of a blank page.

Human-in-the-loop

AI can suggest. Engineers decide.

Aegix is not designed to replace engineering judgement. It is designed to amplify it. AI can identify relevant information, propose relationships, suggest potential failure modes and surface areas that may deserve attention. Engineers review, challenge, modify and approve the analysis.

AI identifies→ Engineer evaluates→ Team validates→ Engineering decision

AI-assisted engineering, not autonomous safety decision-making.

Traceability

Connect the reasoning behind the FMEA.

An FMEA is more than a table of failure modes. It is a chain of engineering reasoning — and Aegix keeps the links, not just the rows.

The FMEA becomes part of the engineering lifecycle, not a document produced in isolation.

Requirement
The system must maintain safe operating conditions.
Function
Maintain operating conditions within defined limits.
Failure mode
Operating condition exceeds the specified limit.
Cause
Temperature sensing failure.
Effect
Incorrect thermal control.
Risk
Potential degradation or safety consequence.
Mitigation
Diagnostic monitoring and protective strategy.
Verification
Test, requirement and evidence.
