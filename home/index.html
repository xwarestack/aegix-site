Traceability

Connect the reasoning behind the FMEA.

An FMEA is more than a table of failure modes. It is a chain of engineering reasoning — and Aegix keeps the links, not just the rows.

RequirementThe system must maintain safe operating conditions.
FunctionMaintain operating conditions within defined limits.
Failure modeOperating condition exceeds the specified limit.
CauseTemperature sensing failure.
EffectIncorrect thermal control.
RiskPotential degradation or safety consequence.
MitigationDiagnostic monitoring and protective strategy.
VerificationTest, requirement and evidence.

The FMEA becomes part of the engineering lifecycle, not a document produced in isolation.
